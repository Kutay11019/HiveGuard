# 🐝 HiveGuard: Honey Empire

Build, expand, and defend your hive in a fast-paced cycle of calm collection and chaotic survival.

---

## 🎮 Overview

**HiveGuard: Honey Empire** is a session-friendly strategy + action game that blends resource collection, base management, and wave-based defense.

By day, you explore vibrant meadows collecting pollen.  
By night, you defend your hive against increasingly dangerous bear attacks.

**Your ultimate goal**
👉 Reach Season 10 and defeat the legendary Great Bear.

---

## 🔄 Core Gameplay Loop

- 🌼 **Day** → Explore and collect pollen    
- 🍯 **Night** → Convert pollen into honey   
- 🐻 **Defense** → Defend against bear waves  
- 💰 **Economy** → Sell and manage resources   
- 🔧 **Progression** → Improve your hive and abilities    
- 🔁 Repeat

Each cycle takes **3–5 minutes**, making the game perfect for short sessions.

---

## 🎯 Player Objective

Grow from a single bee into a powerful hive by:

- Increasing pollen collection efficiency
- Expanding honey production capacity
- Defending against stronger enemies each night
- Managing resources wisely
- Progressing through seasons

🏆 **Win Condition**
- Reach Season 10
- Defeat the Great Bear

❌ **Failure Conditions**

- 🏚️ Hive health reaches zero → Hive collapses (restart season)
- 🐻 Bears steal honey → Partial loss (no revenue carried forward)
- ⚖️ Poor upgrades / resource mismanagement → Progress stalls

---

## 🧠 Design Pillars

### 🎵 Rhythmic Tension
Calm, colorful daytime → intense, dangerous nighttime.

### 📈 Visible Growth
Progress is not just numerical:
- Bigger hive
- Stronger bee
- Expanded honeycombs

### 🎯 Easy to Learn, Hard to Master
- Night 1 → One slow bear  
- Later → Multi-directional attacks + different bear types

### ⚖️ Meaningful Decisions
Every choice matters:
- Upgrade stinger?
- Expand storage?
- Repair hive?

### ⏱️ Session-Friendly
Quick cycles, flexible playtime, no forced long sessions.

---

## 🌍 World & Theme

- 🖼️ **Perspective**: Isometric
- 🎨 **Style**: Cartoonish but detailed
- 🌼 **Daytime**: Bright, lively, colorful meadows
- 🌙 **Nighttime**: Dark, tense, dangerous
- 🌳 **Environment**: Forest clearing with a central wooden hive

A timeless, fairy-tale-like natural setting with no modern elements.

---

## 👤 Player Role
